In Nowhere

In Nowhere is the debut album by the band Ssendas, founded by experienced musicians Iki Capitan (Alan Wilhelm) and Cristián Ifrán.

The songs feature sweet yet dark melodies, drawing from subtle elements of progressive rock, dark rock, hard rock, and even some metal. The band's name, spelled backward as "Sadness", expresses the core emotion the group aims to convey through various themes.

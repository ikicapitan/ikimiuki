Rocking Horse Glittered

Rocking Horse Glittered is my album released in 2026, where I reimagine my first songs written at age 17, which were once objects of ridicule.

It is a work that transforms a basic toy into a profound, satirical, and philosophical piece—a departure from the ordinary, featuring a style and concepts never seen before.
